<p><i>Sidestep</i> opens websites and apps that your internet provider blocks with deep packet inspection (DPI). It runs <a href='https://github.com/hufrea/byedpi' target='_blank' rel='nofollow noopener'>ByeDPI</a> on your device and sends your traffic through it.</p>
<p>Sidestep is not a VPN service. It uses Android's VPN feature to redirect traffic on the device, but nothing is sent to a remote server. It does not encrypt your traffic or hide your IP address.</p>
<p>
<b>Features</b><br>
* One button to connect, with a clear status and hints<br>
* Strategy finder: tests ready-made strategies on your network, scores them and applies the best one in one tap<br>
* Saved strategies that you can switch from the main screen, pin and rename<br>
* Visual editor, or ByeDPI command-line arguments for experts<br>
* VPN mode for all apps, all apps except the ones you choose, or only the ones you choose<br>
* Local SOCKS5 proxy mode for single apps<br>
* Starts when the device turns on; Quick Settings tile and shortcuts<br>
* Works on Android TV and TV boxes<br>
* 18 languages, light and dark themes, colors from your wallpaper on Android 12+<br>
* Import and export of settings (backups from ByeByeDPI work too)<br>
</p>
<p>Sidestep is a redesigned fork of <a href='https://github.com/romanvht/ByeByeDPI' target='_blank' rel='nofollow noopener'>ByeByeDPI</a> by romanvht.</p>
